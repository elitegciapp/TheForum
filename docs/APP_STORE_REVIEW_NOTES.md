# App Store Review Notes — The Forum

## Positioning
- The Forum is a private, invite-only professional community.
- Access is provided via invitation/access codes.
- It is not a public social network; content is intended for members.

## Safety & Moderation
- The Forum prohibits harassment, threats, hate speech, doxxing/private data extraction, and other unsafe conduct.
- Moderation includes content removal, thread locking, feature restrictions, and account termination at administrator discretion.
- No public engagement mechanics (no likes/upvotes/downvotes) and no public “hot”/viral labeling.

## Privacy
- The app provides a plain-language Privacy Summary that discloses minimal data collection and use limited to operating and safeguarding the community.
- The app does not sell personal data.
- The privacy disclosure avoids over-promising deletion/retention guarantees.

## Screenshot & Redistribution
- The app clearly discloses that the community is private and redistribution may result in loss of access.
- The app does not claim perfect detection and does not describe detection methods.
